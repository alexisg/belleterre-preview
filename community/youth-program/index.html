Youth Program

Each summer the Village runs a six-week program at the beach, from the beginning of July through the second week of August, for children ages 4 to 16. It includes an American Red Cross approved swimming course, water safety, and arts and crafts.

Register

Registration details and forms are on the youth program registration page. Program fees can be paid online.

Questions? Contact the Village Office.
